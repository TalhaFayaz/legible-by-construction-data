  Spearman correlation of a set's median-centred log decision time with:

  Per rater, against the spread of defect counts inside the set:
    R1: rho=-0.434 (p=0.000139)
    R2: rho=-0.212 (p=0.0732)
    R3: rho=-0.210 (p=0.0765)

  Sets that contain a model-placed figure (n=58) are decided 1.98 times faster
  than sets of four engine layouts (n=14): medians +0.034 against +0.719 in
  centred log seconds, Mann-Whitney p=8.4e-05. The raters' own response times say
  that a broken layout is easy to reject and that the two engines are hard to separate,
  which is the comparison the study could not resolve.
