== W2  Equivalence bounds for the tied verdicts ==

A non-significant Wilcoxon test does not show equivalence. For every
comparison reported as tied we give the two one-sided bootstrap test
(TOST) bound: the largest difference compatible with the data at 90%
confidence, that is max(|lo|,|hi|) of the 90% interval of the mean
paired difference. Equivalence at a margin d holds when that bound < d.
Margins: 0.02 of the graph score (the smallest difference this paper
reports as significant is 0.023) and 5 judge points of 100.

comparison                                      n   mean d                90% CI   bound  verdict
SciFlow-Bench / Qwen / ours vs dot            483   +0.003      [-0.003, +0.010]   0.010  equivalent at 0.02; equivalent at 0.01
FlowVQA / Gemma / ours vs dot                 300   +0.005      [-0.010, +0.021]   0.021  inconclusive at 0.02; equivalent at 0.025
FlowVQA / Qwen / ours vs dot_matched          300   +0.009      [-0.006, +0.023]   0.023  inconclusive at 0.02; equivalent at 0.025
FlowVQA / Ministral / ours vs dot_matched     300   +0.009      [-0.005, +0.024]   0.024  inconclusive at 0.02; equivalent at 0.025
PaperBanana / Gemma / ours vs dot_matched     292   +1.855      [-1.370, +5.051]   5.051  inconclusive at 5; equivalent at 6

sensitivity: the smallest margin at which each case is equivalent is its bound.
