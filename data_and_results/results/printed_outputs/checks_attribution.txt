  One criterion bounds both engines. An arrow crossing a label is in 90.6% of our
  remaining failures and the only thing wrong with 62.4% of them, and in 76.3% of
  dot's; the three lenient criteria never fail for our engine, and dot fails one of
  them on 1.8% of its failures. So the defect class that separates the engines (A1)
  is also the one that limits the better of them, and it is a label stage that may
  move a label off its own route -- a leader line or a reserved channel -- that
  addresses it, not a better placement of boxes. Nothing else is left above 26%.
