B8  WHY THE DIAGRAMEVAL-STYLE COLUMNS ARE NOT REPORTED  (negative result)
==============================================================================
pipeline_items.csv carries a second metric family under strict matching
(strict_node_f1, strict_path_f1, strict_diagrameval_score, ...). We checked
whether it could carry a second reading of the results. It cannot:

  flowvqa  items whose paths are not comparable: 72 of 2700 (3%), and their
           strict_path_f1 is 1.000 by convention, not by measurement.
  sciflow  items whose paths are not comparable: 1367 of 4347 (31%), and their
           strict_path_f1 is 1.000 by convention, not by measurement.

Since strict_diagrameval_score is the mean of node and path F1, that default
propagates: on SciFlow-Bench it correlates with the benchmark score at
  flowvqa  rho = +0.680 over all items, +0.692 over the comparable ones
  sciflow  rho = -0.222 over all items, +0.212 over the comparable ones

The apparent disagreement between the two metric families is an artefact of
the default, and disappears once incomparable items are excluded. The family
is therefore not used in the paper. The same columns do carry one usable
check: scoring our own graphs with exact label matching instead of the
benchmark's 0.5 cosine threshold ranks items the same way,
  flowvqa  Gemma      rho = +0.745
  flowvqa  Ministral  rho = +0.745
  flowvqa  Qwen       rho = +0.760
  sciflow  Gemma      rho = +0.805
  sciflow  Ministral  rho = +0.826
  sciflow  Qwen       rho = +0.806
